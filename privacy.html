privacy policy

last updated november 7, 2026

short version: this site doesn't use cookies, doesn't run ads, and doesn't sell or share anything about you. here's everything that happens when you visit.

visit counts

coobyp.com uses GoatCounter to count visits. it sets no cookies and doesn't track you across sites. it records the page you viewed, the site that linked you here, your browser and screen size, and a rough country. your IP address is used to tell unique visits apart and isn't stored. see GoatCounter's privacy page.

music player

the player streams from SoundCloud. when it loads, your browser connects to SoundCloud, and SoundCloud's own privacy policy applies to that, including any cookies they set.

fonts & hosting

fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to fetch them (Google's privacy policy). the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep server logs including IP addresses for security (GitHub's privacy statement).

links out

links to soundcloud, youtube, instagram, x, snapchat, bluesky, github, discord and the merch store take you to those sites, and their policies apply there.

email

if you email me@coobyp.com, i get your address and message and use them only to reply. nothing gets added to a mailing list.

questions

hit me at me@coobyp.com.
